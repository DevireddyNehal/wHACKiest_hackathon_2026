How it works

Three parts, one interaction

  1. 1

    Gaze

    Identifies the likely target. The demo uses pointer movement as a stand-in.

  2. 2

    Voice

    Says what to do, such as open, scroll, select, or go back.

  3. 3

    Confirmation

    Confirms the selected target visually and with text before acting.

Supported in this demo

Working controls

  • Pointer/gaze stand-in
  • Target hover and dwell
  • Browser speech recognition
  • Scroll and back commands
  • Camera preview via browser permission
Prototype limits

What it cannot do yet

  • No calibrated real eye tracking
  • No guaranteed speech recognition support
  • No browser-wide control outside this page
  • No remote monitoring or cloud upload
  • No medical, safety, or emergency use
